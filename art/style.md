# Style block

Paste verbatim into every image prompt.

```
STYLE: casual mobile cartoon game art. Chunky rounded friendly shapes, thick clean dark-brown outline (#2A1A14) of even weight around the silhouette and every main shape, flat cel shading with exactly two tones per color plus one small soft highlight, light coming from the top-left, no gradients, no noise, no texture, no painterly brush strokes. Bright saturated cheerful palette: sky blue #7EC8F2, grass green #6BBF3A, dark green #3E8E2A, soil brown #9A6B3F, jersey green #2EAD4B, cape red #E0342F, princess pink #FF9CC8, lilac #B79CFF, violet #7B5CD6, gold #FFC83D, white #FFFFFF, skin #FFD7B5, outline #2A1A14. Characters are cute little kids with chibi proportions: the head is about 45 percent of total height, big expressive eyes, small round body, short limbs. Pure side view facing right, camera at character height, orthographic look, no perspective distortion.
```

# Mandatory requirements

Append to every sprite prompt:

```
REQUIREMENTS: single object, centered, full body visible, generous empty padding on all sides, solid pure #FF00FF magenta background filling the whole image, no shadow on the background, no ground, no text, no watermark, no border, side view facing right.
```
